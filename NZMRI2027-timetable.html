Wednesday 13 January — Abel Tasman National Park

Bus: departs at 8:30am and returns at approximately 5:30pm.

We are organising a day trip to Abel Tasman National Park. Once we arrive there is no organised itinerary, so participants are free to explore the park at their own pace.

Possible activities include taking a water taxi to a bay further along the coast and hiking back along the coastal track, renting a kayak to explore the coastline, or simply walking and relaxing on one of the beaches.

Please do a little research beforehand, arrange any necessary bookings in advance, and consider teaming up with other participants to explore the park in small groups.
